# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primarily **tourists planning a visit** to Marche-en-Famenne and the Famenne region: holidaymakers and prospective visitors deciding what to see, where to eat, where to sleep, what to do, and how to fill a day or a stay. They arrive in discovery mode (often before booking anything) and want to be inspired and oriented quickly. A secondary audience of locals and day-trippers uses the same surfaces for events and weekend ideas, but trip-planning discovery is the dominant job the design serves.

The site is multilingual (fr / en / nl / de via WPML), so visitors land in different languages and layouts must hold up with longer translated strings.

## Product Purpose

Visit Marche is the official tourism destination site for Marche-en-Famenne. It turns the region's offer (curated WordPress content plus live data from the Pivot tourism API) into an inspiring, browsable experience organized around clear intents: **visiter, manger, dormir, bouger, s'inspirer**. Success looks like a visitor who arrives curious and leaves with a concrete plan: a place to stay, things to do, and a reason to stay longer.

## Brand Personality

**Warm, inviting, regional.** Welcoming and human, with the texture of a real place rather than a generic destination. Voice is friendly and encouraging, in the first/second person ("et si vous viviez l'expérience complète ?"), never corporate or sales-y. The current palette carries this: a soft cream ground, coral and teal CTAs, warm category hues, real photography of the town and countryside. Warmth comes from imagery, color, and copy, not from gimmicks.

Three words: **welcoming, rooted, unhurried.**

## Anti-references

This should NOT look or feel like:

- **Generic SaaS / AI template** — flat repeating card grids, gradient hero blobs, tracked-eyebrow scaffolding, cookie-cutter startup sameness.
- **Cold corporate / civic** — stiff municipal-government tone: bureaucratic, gray, form-heavy, joyless.
- **Cluttered booking portal / OTA** — the Booking/TripAdvisor density: ad-heavy, noisy, aggressive cross-sell, pushy CTAs everywhere.
- **Loud & gimmicky** — garish neon, heavy gratuitous animation, pop-ups stacked on pop-ups, carnival energy.

## Design Principles

1. **Inspire before you organize.** Lead with imagery and place; let the intent categories (visiter/manger/dormir/bouger/s'inspirer) carry the structure underneath. Discovery first, taxonomy second.
2. **Warmth is earned, not decorated.** Carry the welcoming feel through real photography, the regional palette, and human copy, not through effects or ornament.
3. **One clear next step per surface.** A visitor should always see the obvious thing to do next (explore a category, open an offer, plan a stay) without competing CTAs.
4. **Calm density.** Generous, unhurried layouts over portal-style clutter; restraint is part of the brand, not a limitation.
5. **Speak the visitor's language, in every language.** Copy is warm and concrete in fr/en/nl/de; designs must stay intact when strings get longer in translation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: body text ≥ 4.5:1 and large text ≥ 3:1 contrast (watch muted grays and soft category hues on cream), full keyboard navigation with visible focus states, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for any motion, and proper labels/roles on interactive and media elements. Layouts must tolerate the longer strings that fr/en/nl/de translation produces without overflow or truncation.
